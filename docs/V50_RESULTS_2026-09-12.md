## Mechanistic interpretation

The frozen non-overlap implementation groups by `venue × symbol`, orders candidates by `entry_time` and `signal_time`, keeps the earliest candidate, and blocks later candidates until the retained candidate exits.

It does **not** use predicted Expected-R, lower Expected-R, state probabilities, or economic utility to arbitrate between overlapping candidates.

v0.50 does not prove that any specific alternative arbitration rule is superior. It does establish that, in the consumed development evidence, the current earliest-first non-overlap transformation is the only pipeline layer that satisfies the preregistered broad-harm attribution rule.

This result narrows the next research question substantially: the next stage should investigate **overlap-conflict arbitration** prospectively, while keeping the predictor, costs and Financial Governor fixed.
