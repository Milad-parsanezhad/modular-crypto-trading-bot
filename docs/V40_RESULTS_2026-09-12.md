## Conformal-admission finding

The admission bottleneck is not merely the frozen `p_win >= 0.55` threshold.

For `V40_HISTGB_HURDLE`:

- about 1,677 OOS rows reached `p_win >= 0.55`;
- about 7,159 had point expected R > 0;
- **zero** had regime-aware lower expected R > 0.

For `V40_LOGIT_HUBER_HURDLE`:

- about 1,473 reached `p_win >= 0.55`;
- about 6,513 had point expected R > 0;
- only `5` had lower expected R > 0;
- those five had negative realized OOS economics.

Therefore simply lowering the probability threshold would not solve the scientific problem. The conservative lower-bound failure is driven by weak predictive discrimination and large residual uncertainty.

## Difference from v0.39

v0.39 established that a single-stage regression faced a bias/variance/admission frontier.

v0.40 tested whether separating:

`trade probability -> payoff magnitude -> uncertainty`

would solve that problem.

It did not.

The new evidence localizes the bottleneck earlier:

`mother event -> current feature representation / target definition -> Stage-1 discrimination`

The current features do not reliably distinguish profitable from unprofitable mother events out of sample.

## Risk-system finding

The capital governor again behaved conservatively. Only two trades reached financial execution in the Logit candidate, and account drawdown remained extremely small. The failure is therefore not caused by excessive risk-taking.

## Anti-overfitting decision

The following are explicitly prohibited under v0.40 after observing these results:

- lowering `p_win` below 0.55;
- relaxing or removing the positive conformal lower-bound requirement;
- changing conformal alpha;
- choosing the best seed;
- deleting weak folds/quarters/regimes/venues;
- changing the mother-strategy feature vector under the same experiment ID;
- adding PatchTST/CMamba under v0.40;
- increasing leverage/risk;
- touching Kraken.

## Scientific implication for the next experiment

The evidence no longer supports the hypothesis that model capacity is the primary limitation.

A future experiment should investigate the **representation and target construction** before increasing capacity. Defensible directions include, under a new preregistration:

1. event-type-specific models rather than pooling all mother events into one label distribution;
2. separate long/short conditional models if preregistered;
3. path-aware targets that distinguish stop/target/time exits rather than collapsing them immediately to binary `net_r > 0`;
4. competing-risk/survival modeling of time-to-stop versus time-to-target;
5. stronger causal microstructure/on-chain inputs only where timestamped historical availability can be proven;
6. regime-specific experts with an independently causal regime gate rather than post-hoc regime filtering.

These are future hypotheses only. They are not authorized modifications of v0.40.
