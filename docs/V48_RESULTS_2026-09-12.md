## What v0.48 tested

The preregistered hypothesis was that the remaining v0.47 fold instability might be attributable to temporal distribution shift / within-regime non-stationarity.

The test used all `120` canonical C1 fold×symbol units and compared:

- internal CAL drift (`CAL_EARLY -> CAL_LATE`) against forward drift (`CAL_LATE -> TEST`);
- standardized 1-Wasserstein distance over the frozen `V41_FEATURES`;
- Jensen-Shannon divergence for R1 state, common outcome, regime, event family, side and venue;
- one-sided exact sign tests by fold with Benjamini-Hochberg correction at `q <= 0.10`;
- fold-cluster bootstrap Spearman linkage between continuous drift and canonical v0.47 forecast metrics.

No trading filter was built from any result.

## Scientific interpretation

v0.48 falsifies a simple next-step story:

> “The model fails mainly because the marginal input distribution drifts between calibration and test.”

The frozen evidence does not support that as a broad explanation.

A more plausible unresolved mechanism is **conditional instability** rather than marginal distribution shift: the same observable feature/state combinations may map to different outcome/economic distributions across time, venues or sides, even when marginal distributions do not move enough to trigger drift diagnostics.

This points toward a next diagnostic focused on conditional relationships / interaction stability rather than another static calibrator, larger model, asset deletion, regime deletion or venue deletion.
