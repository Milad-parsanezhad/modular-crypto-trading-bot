## 7. Central scientific finding

v0.39 identifies a clear bias–variance / admission frontier:

- Ridge: low variance but excessive conservatism / insufficient admitted sample;
- HistGB: intermediate complexity, modest positive venue-level means, but uncertainty and temporal robustness remain insufficient;
- shallow MLP: more selected observations, but OOS expectancy and seed stability deteriorate.

Therefore simply increasing model capacity to PatchTST/CMamba is **not scientifically justified by this run**.

The evidence does not say "a bigger neural network is needed." It says the current single-stage regression + globally calibrated lower-bound admission does not simultaneously achieve sufficient sample, temporal persistence and robust positive economics.

## 12. Next scientifically valid hypothesis

The evidence supports testing a new **two-stage / hurdle decision architecture**, not simply a larger network:

1. **Stage A — meta-label / admission model**
   - estimate whether a mother-strategy event has positive post-cost economic value;
   - explicitly handle the large mass of low-quality candidate events.

2. **Stage B — conditional net-R model**
   - estimate R only conditional on Stage-A admission;
   - separate occurrence probability from payoff magnitude.

3. **Regime-conditional uncertainty calibration**
   - calibrate uncertainty prospectively by frozen regime groups rather than one global residual buffer;
   - retain strict causality and minimum calibration sample requirements.

4. **Survival / duration head**
   - predict capital-lock duration and failure/hit timing;
   - restore the survival-aware IRGC-S lineage as a formal model component.

5. **Same financial governor and same OOS gates**
   - risk limits remain unchanged;
   - no threshold relaxation;
   - Kraken remains sealed until a genuine development winner exists.

This should be registered as a new experiment (recommended: `v0.40`), not as a retuned v0.39.
