# v0.19 → v0.20 Research Plan

Date: 2026-09-10

Status: **Registered plan; no strategy promotion**

## Research objective

The next scientific objective is not to maximize backtest return. It is to determine whether a high-integrity prospective microstructure measurement layer can add robust 4h predictive/economic information beyond the existing price/Ichimoku baseline family.

## Phase Q — prospective data-quality pilot

**Input:** 30-minute target-cadence public REST snapshots for BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT from CoinEx, OKX and KuCoin.

**Frozen measurement contract:**

- primary decision horizon remains 4h;
- reported-trade PIT window = 60 seconds ending at the order-book timestamp;
- public provider `side` is labelled `exchange_reported_side_unverified_aggressor`;
- exact quote-notional depth by level;
- maximum cross-venue clock skew = 60s;
- maximum recent-trade staleness = 30s;
- minimum recent trades per accepted venue = 5;
- minimum accepted venues per symbol = 2.

**Maturity:** >=168 elapsed hours, >=336 nominal measurement opportunities and >=80% authorized coverage for both BTC and ETH. Manual reruns and duplicate hashes do not increase coverage.

**Deliverable:** `quality_gate.json` generated by `scripts/evaluate_v19_quality_gate.py`.

## Phase F — frozen 4h feature construction

Only after Phase Q passes, create a one-row-per-symbol-per-4h-decision table from measurements available **before or at** the decision clock. No target is inspected while choosing the feature aggregation rule.

Candidate feature families to pre-register before evaluation:

- liquidity level: median/robust mean spread and depth;
- within-window variability: dispersion of spread/depth imbalance;
- reported flow: robust mean/median reported trade imbalance;
- agreement: fraction of measurements with cross-venue sign agreement;
- drift: pre-specified within-4h slope in mid dispersion/flow;
- coverage: minimum/mean accepted-venue count as a quality covariate, not alpha by itself.

The exact subset and transformations must be frozen in a new protocol before predictive labels are joined.

## Phase P — predictive ablation

Primary comparison:

`PRICE_ONLY` vs `PRICE_PLUS_MICROSTRUCTURE`

Requirements:

- >=250 independent 4h decision timestamps after filtering;
- next-4h target frozen in advance;
- purged chronological evaluation or future holdout;
- explicit fees, turnover, spread/slippage sensitivity;
- no hidden search outside the trial registry;
- paired dependence-aware bootstrap;
- FDR across planned comparisons;
- SPA / White Reality Check and DSR/PSR if multiple adaptive candidates are searched;
- asset-level and market-regime diagnostics reported but not post-hoc optimized.

Promotion requires positive **net** incremental value, not classification accuracy alone.

## Phase E — execution relevance

If Phase P supports incremental value, the candidate enters a fresh PAPER shadow comparison. Existing independent risk, kill-switch and execution-reconciliation layers remain mandatory.

No direct transition from retrospective research to LIVE is permitted.

## Conditional v0.20 — WebSocket/L2 measurement upgrade

A higher-resolution event collector is justified only if the REST pilot shows one or more of:

- persistent provider-side truncation of the 60-second window;
- insufficient temporal coverage despite healthy markets;
- unstable side semantics or stale trade history;
- evidence that snapshot-only depth misses material within-window dynamics;
- a scientifically compelling short-horizon hypothesis requiring event timing/order-book updates.

If triggered, v0.20 should collect short-burst exchange-native WebSocket/L2 events with sequence/checksum handling and explicit gap detection. That stage must be registered as a **new data source**, not silently mixed with v0.19.

## Stop rules

Stop or redesign the hypothesis rather than endlessly tuning when:

- Gate Q fails for structural provider reasons after reasonable adapter fixes;
- the fixed microstructure family has non-positive incremental OOS net edge;
- confidence intervals do not support the claimed direction;
- benefit appears only in one asset or one post-hoc regime outside the stated scope;
- realistic trading frictions remove the effect;
- search-aware tests indicate likely data-snooping/selection bias.

Negative outcomes remain thesis results.
