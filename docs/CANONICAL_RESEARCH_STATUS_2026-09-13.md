## Why the scientific lineage remains stacked

The post-v0.25 research chain contains many preregistration -> empirical -> diagnostic transitions. Flattening hundreds of research commits into one default-branch merge would obscure:

- which hypothesis was frozen before outcome inspection;
- which engineering fixes occurred before versus after a valid decision;
- which runs/artifacts are canonical versus superseded;
- which data sources are consumed versus fresh;
- the exact negative-result trail required for the thesis defense.

Therefore `main` is the **canonical navigation and deployment branch**, while exact scientific heads, artifacts and preregistration commits remain authoritative for individual experiments.
