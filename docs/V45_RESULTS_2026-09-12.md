## Executive diagnosis

The v0.44 failure is not explained by the event clock alone. v0.45 localizes the current bottleneck to a combination of:

1. **weak TARGET/STOP separability in the frozen feature representation**;
2. **substantial probability miscalibration with only small gains in resolution over the empirical baseline**;
3. **a label-process imbalance in which STOP dominates TARGET and a large TIME mass remains economically meaningful**;
4. **conditional pockets of information that are not globally stable enough to justify post-hoc feature/family selection**.

This result does not promote any feature, regime, event family, threshold or model.

## Important TIME outcome finding

TIME outcomes are not economically neutral in this sample.

Among 27,826 TIME events:

- duration is exactly 30 bars by construction;
- mean net R ≈ **+0.5104R**;
- median net R ≈ **+0.4251R**;
- approximately **72.04%** have positive net R.

This is an important diagnostic observation. v0.41/v0.44 already include a timeout-value head, so it would be incorrect to claim that TIME value was wholly ignored. However, the cause-specific hazard problem and the frozen `P(target) > P(stop)` admission condition operate on a label process where many economically positive paths terminate as TIME rather than TARGET. A future experiment may therefore need to test a more economically aligned terminal-state/representation formulation under a new preregistration rather than merely increasing model capacity.
