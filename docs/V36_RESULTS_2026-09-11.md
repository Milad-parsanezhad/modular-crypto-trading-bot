## Interpretation

v0.36 materially improved the research state even though it did not promote a candidate. Direct expected-net-R modelling with nonlinear HistGradientBoosting produced a cross-venue economic signal that was far stronger than the rejected v0.35 binary/asymmetric meta-label family. The remaining blocker is not average profitability; it is **block stability / temporal confidence on CoinEx**.

This is a useful distinction for the thesis:

1. predictive/economic edge exists in the nonlinear event-ranking arm across all three consumed venues;
2. evidence is not yet statistically stable enough across time blocks to promote;
3. portfolio implementation still has to be tested independently rather than inferred from event-level PF.
