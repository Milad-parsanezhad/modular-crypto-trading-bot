# AI Cryptocurrency Trading Bot Research

This is the documentation landing page for **Modular Crypto Trading Bot**, an MSc-level research platform for **AI cryptocurrency trading**, **financial machine learning**, **deep learning**, **algorithmic trading**, **portfolio optimization** and future **reinforcement-learning allocation**.

The project emphasizes causal data, realistic costs, purged validation, model-search controls, external/future replication, and mark-to-market/CVaR portfolio risk rather than promotional backtest claims.

## Current research frontier

The latest completed external experiment found that frozen event-level ML filters retained positive event economics on two venues, while portfolio-level risk/admission gates failed. The active research direction therefore studies **risk-aware learning-to-rank for simultaneous trade admission**, followed by genuinely future-time validation.
