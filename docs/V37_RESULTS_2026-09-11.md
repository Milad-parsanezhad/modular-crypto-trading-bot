## What v0.37 teaches us

The experiment cleanly separates two effects:

1. **Risk control worked:** v0.37 reduced worst development drawdown to well below the 5% hard gate for the leading HistGB family.
2. **Admission quality did not yet work:** the fixed max-4/single-symbol/correlation-CVaR arbitration sharply reduced event count and did not preserve robust PF/breadth on OKX.

This means the earlier portfolio problem cannot be described merely as insufficient risk suppression. The current admission policy itself changes which events survive, and on OKX that selection destroys enough economic quality to fail the gate.

The next research route should therefore not loosen `min_trades=200`, breadth, PF, CI or risk caps. A defensible next hypothesis would change the portfolio-allocation mechanism itself—e.g. soft differentiable risk allocation / constrained optimization over simultaneous events rather than hard top-k admission—while preserving the successful v0.36 nonlinear expected-net-R signal as a research feature, not a promoted model.
