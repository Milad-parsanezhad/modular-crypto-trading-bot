## Methodological audit: nominal seed stability is not sufficient evidence

During artifact review, multiple HistGradientBoosting fold/seed outputs were identical or nearly identical. The current sklearn HistGradientBoosting configuration uses `early_stopping=False`; its `random_state` is not a reliable independent robustness perturbation of the learned hypothesis in this setup. Therefore, nominal estimator seed agreement must not be interpreted as substantive stability evidence.

This does not change the v0.42 decision (v0.42 already fails temporal and economic gates). It changes the design of the **next experiment only**: v0.43 preregisters deterministic moving-block perturbations of the *training data* and uses median/2-of-3 perturbation agreement instead of relying on estimator seed labels.
