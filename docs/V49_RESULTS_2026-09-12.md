## Scientific interpretation

v0.48 showed that broad marginal distribution shift was not sufficiently supported. v0.49 now shows that a second simple explanation is also unsupported:

> “The v0.47 instability is mainly caused by a broad time-varying conditional mapping P(Y|X) that can be observed as calibration-map drift, feature→residual interaction drift or forecast-loss change across most folds.”

The frozen evidence does not support this statement.

This is an important narrowing of the research problem. Temporal instability in economic performance remains real, but neither broad marginal drift nor broad conditional-drift diagnostics explain it.

The evidence now points away from another generic drift detector or adaptive model as the immediate next move.

## Implication for the next research question

A more plausible unresolved bottleneck is the **mapping from predicted states/probabilities to economic utility and portfolio admission** rather than broad forecast-distribution instability.

The next research stage should first diagnose, without changing trading policy:

1. stability of FIT-estimated state economic means when applied to CAL/TEST;
2. calibration of predicted expected-R against realized net-R;
3. whether ranking by predicted expected-R preserves realized-R ordering;
4. whether economic failures arise before or after non-overlap / Financial Governor admission;
5. whether state-probability quality and economic-utility quality decouple by fold, venue, side or event family.

This should be an **expected-R / utility-mapping failure-attribution diagnostic**, not a new larger learner.
