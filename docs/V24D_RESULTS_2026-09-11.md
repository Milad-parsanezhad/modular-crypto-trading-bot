## 6. Post-outcome diagnostic discovery — exploratory only

The external result exposed a useful architecture mismatch. This subsection is explicitly **post-hoc diagnostic** and cannot rescue or promote v0.24d.

The frozen event-level filter improved net event economics on both venues, including under 36/60 bps stress and broad cross-sectional coverage, yet the overlap-aware MTM portfolio admitted a subset whose economics collapsed.

A major mechanism is visible in the current allocator:

- simultaneous candidates are ordered deterministically by `entry_time`, then `strategy`, then `symbol`;
- the frozen meta score is used as a binary gate but **not as admission priority** once portfolio/strategy/concurrency caps bind;
- portfolio constraints therefore choose among simultaneous positive-filter events using an economically arbitrary alphabetical tie-break;
- after the 5% MTM kill triggers, many later selected opportunities are necessarily rejected.

## 7. Scientific interpretation

v0.24d falsifies the claim that the exact frozen family-meta filter, combined with the existing deterministic portfolio allocator, is ready for promotion.

It does **not** establish that the event scores contain no information. In fact, the cross-venue event-level evidence suggests a narrower hypothesis worth researching: positive candidate selection may exist, while the portfolio admission/arbitration layer destroys or fails to capture that incremental value under clustered signals and hard risk limits.

That narrower hypothesis is now a **new Plan-D research problem** and must consume new fresh evidence after redesign.

## 8. Next research direction

The next stage should separate:

1. **event alpha / abstention** — is a candidate worth considering?
2. **portfolio arbitration** — among simultaneous candidates, which should receive scarce risk budget?
3. **duration / capital lock-up** — how long is risk likely to remain occupied?
4. **uncertainty** — when is the model insufficiently reliable to rank candidates?
5. **marginal portfolio risk** — correlation, CVaR and existing exposures at admission time.

Research candidates for the redesigned allocator include:

- learning-to-rank rather than independent binary classification;
- lower-confidence-bound / conformal uncertainty-aware admission;
- survival/duration-aware utility;
- score calibration to expected net R;
- correlation/CVaR-aware marginal utility;
- a future RL allocator only after deterministic/ML ranking baselines pass.

The redesigned policy must be frozen and then evaluated on genuinely future-time evidence; the v0.24d external sample becomes **development/diagnostic evidence only** for the new hypothesis.
