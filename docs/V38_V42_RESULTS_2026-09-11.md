Date: 2026-09-11

This document records the five preregistered stages executed after v0.37. The umbrella preregistration was posted to PR #17 before the new results were inspected. ## Scientific conclusion

The five-stage chain separates two questions that had previously been entangled: whether a predictive event model contains economic signal, and whether that signal can survive portfolio admission, time, venue changes and execution frictions. v0.36 had shown a promising HistGB event-level near-miss, but v0.38 demonstrated that the tested soft allocation did not preserve that edge on OKX or retain sufficient sample size. v0.39 demonstrated temporal instability, and v0.40 showed that realistic cost stress does not explain the main failure: CoinEx and KuCoin remained profitable under severe stress while OKX remained structurally weak. v0.41 showed that the execution-control architecture can be made fail-closed and auditable independently of strategy quality. v0.42 correctly prevented scientific weakness from being converted into deployment authorization.

The appropriate next research action is therefore not to lower gates or start real trading. It is to formulate a genuinely new alpha/portfolio hypothesis that specifically targets cross-venue and temporal invariance while preserving the sealed Kraken holdout. Kraken has not been used in v0.38–v0.42 and remains available for a future single locked candidate.
