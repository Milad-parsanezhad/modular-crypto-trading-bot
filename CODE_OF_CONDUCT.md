# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository is an academic and engineering research environment. Discussion should help improve correctness, reproducibility, clarity and safety.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and reviewers should:

- critique methods, code and evidence rather than people;
- distinguish measured results from interpretation or speculation;
- disclose uncertainty, failed experiments and known limitations;
- provide reproducible details when reporting bugs or scientific claims;
- respect differing levels of experience and explain technical objections constructively;
- protect credentials, private data and security-sensitive information;
- avoid promotional or guaranteed-profit language that is not supported by evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, credential sharing, fabricated research evidence, or knowingly misleading performance claims are not acceptable.

## Research-integrity expectations

Scientific disagreement is welcome. When challenging a result, prefer a concrete counterexample, reproduction, test, citation or alternative hypothesis. Negative results are legitimate project outcomes and must not be hidden to make the repository look more successful.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect participants or the integrity/security of the project.
