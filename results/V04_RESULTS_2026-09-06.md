## Evaluation design
- Target: next 4h CoinEx spot return > one-turnover fee+slippage hurdle.
- Fixed model: HistGradientBoostingClassifier; no test-set hyperparameter tuning.
- Expanding walk-forward validation with purge gap.
- Long/flat execution with fixed probability threshold 0.56.
- Fee = 10 bps; slippage = 2 bps per turnover.
- Predictive metrics: AUC, Brier score, log loss.
- Economic metrics: net return, Sharpe, max drawdown, exposure, turnover.
- Incremental inference: paired moving-block bootstrap of strategy-return differences plus Benjamini-Hochberg multiple-testing adjustment.
- Every variant within a panel was evaluated on the same dates.

## Regime-instability clue
Order Flow and Basis helped strongly in the first walk-forward fold but not consistently in later folds. Example Sharpe values:
- Baseline fold 1: +0.71; Order Flow: +1.65; Funding+Basis+OrderFlow: +3.26.
- Later folds were negative for all variants, and Order Flow became strongly negative in fold 5.

This is not evidence of stable alpha; it is a clue that any information content may be **regime-dependent** rather than stationary.

## Scientific conclusion
1. **No v0.4 feature family passes the pre-specified incremental-alpha gate.**
2. Funding: no incremental evidence in the current 4h next-bar formulation.
3. OI: no incremental evidence in the current one-year recent panel.
4. Basis: aggregate point estimate improved, but uncertainty is too large; status = inconclusive.
5. Order Flow proxy: largest positive Delta Sharpe among isolated families (+0.256), but not statistically robust after block bootstrap / multiple-testing adjustment; status = inconclusive.
6. The entire strategy family remains loss-making after costs. Therefore no profitability or live-trading claim is permitted.
7. The fold instability argues for **pre-specified regime-conditional tests**, better economic horizons, calibrated trade/no-trade decisions, and richer same-venue microstructure rather than simply increasing model complexity.
