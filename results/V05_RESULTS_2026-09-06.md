## Method

- Horizons: 4h / 12h / 24h only.
- Outer expanding walk-forward.
- Model fit, Platt calibration and probability-threshold selection performed inside outer-training data only.
- Horizon observations are non-overlapping.
- Round-trip trading friction = 2 × (10 bps fee + 2 bps slippage) per selected event.
- Paired moving-block bootstrap + Benjamini-Hochberg correction.
- Coverage-matched baseline diagnostic for selective-prediction illusions.
- Regime-transition audit.
- CPCV sanity panels, Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO) and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) diagnostics.

## Book-derived research insight discovered during v0.5
The official public repository for Stefan Jansen's *Machine Learning for Trading, 3rd Edition* contains a crypto-perpetual funding case study that frames funding as a **cross-sectional crowding signal across 19 perpetuals at the 8-hour funding settlement clock**, not as a single-BTC next-4h feature. It uses completed-bar timing, official funding cash flows, transaction costs, multiple feature families, fold-specific GARCH/HMM features and later model/backtest/risk/cost stages.

This materially changes the next hypothesis: v0.4/v0.5 failure of single-BTC Funding does not prove Funding is uninformative; the economic formulation may be wrong. A new cross-sectional settlement-aligned replication is warranted.

## Scientific conclusion
